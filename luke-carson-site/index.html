About

I build partnerships between brands and artists. As Head of Special Projects at David Carson Design, I source and secure each collaboration, execute the go-to-market strategy, and lead creative direction when asked, working closely with both the artist and the brand at every stage.

I’m passionate about brand strategy and creative direction, especially pairing brands with other brands and artists. The goal is always a collaboration that feels genuine and naturally connected.
